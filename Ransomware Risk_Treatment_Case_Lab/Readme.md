# GRC103 Module 3 — Ransomware Risk Treatment Case Lab

**Case:** Northbridge Community Health (fictional three-clinic provider)  
**Incident reference:** 2021 ransomware attack on Ireland's Health Service Executive (HSE)  
**Assessment:** Lab 2 — Risk Treatment Strategies (100 marks)  
**Author:** Adediran Eunice Adebukola

## Submission deliverables

The assessment requires **two artifacts**:

1. `GRC103_Lab2_HSE_Risk_Treatment_Report.pdf` — a report of no more than four pages, covering the four-risk register, initial and revised treatment portfolio, transfer/acceptance decisions, 30-day treatment plan, and board memo.
2. `GRC103_Lab2_Risk_Treatment_Decisions.xlsx` — an editable risk register, treatment budget, and illustrative cyber-insurance calculations.

**Note:** These two binary deliverables must be committed separately; this README does not signify that the files are already present.

## Scenario and decision points

- First-year budget ceiling: **$100,000**.
- Four risk domains: service disruption, sensitive-data loss, unnecessary supplier exposure, and delayed detection.
- Initial portfolio under assessment: **A + B + C + E + G**, costing **$90,000**.
- Changed circumstances: restoration test fails, paper bookings last only four hours, and the cost of B rises to $35,000.
- Revised portfolio under assessment: **A + C + D + E + G**, costing **$100,000**. MFA is deferred and remains an escalated risk, not an accepted or eliminated risk.
- Cyber-insurance example: $25,000 deductible, $250,000 maximum insurer payout for a covered claim; exclusions remain fully borne by the organisation.
